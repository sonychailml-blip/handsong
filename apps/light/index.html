How to play

Right hand: raise or lower it to pick a row of notes, pinch a finger to the thumb to play a note.

Move it left or right for volume, nearer or farther for reverb.

Left hand: each finger is an effect — pinch it and move up or down.

Take your hands out of view to see the buttons.

Your camera picture never leaves this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
