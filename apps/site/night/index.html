Handsong Night

Quiet, living music you play with your hands — in a night forest that answers every note with light.

Play Night

Chrome or Safari · a laptop, or a phone on a stand · the camera image never leaves your device

Who it's for

For anyone who wants a quiet evening, a rest after work, or a play with music without ever having studied it. No notes, no knowledge needed.

What you do

Raise a hand and bring your thumb to another finger — a note sounds. Higher hand, higher note. Your other hand shapes the echo and the sound, and your distance from the camera shapes the depth of the space.

What awaits you

A living background music that never repeats, a stream, wind and crickets. Fireflies gather round your light, rings spread on the water, the night slowly changes.

Features

  • Three music moods: Still, Drift, Shimmer
  • Four sounds: firefly, breathy flute, handpan, singing bowl
  • A sleep timer: the music fades by itself
  • Front or back camera, mirroring to a TV
  • Full screen and Add to Home Screen
  • English and Russian

The first note is a few seconds away.
