Handsong Install

A musical installation where visitors play with their hands in the air and the space answers with light and sound. No complex equipment, no sensors.

Discuss a pilot

For which spaces

Museums and galleries

Science, music and children's museums, exhibitions, museum nights.

Events and festivals

Light festivals, conferences, corporate evenings, presentations.

Hotels, spas and lounges

A gentle living screen in a lounge that invites people to come and play.

Children's centres

Family spaces and sensory rooms, for joy and rest.

What you need

  • A computer with a modern browser
  • A webcam — we'll help you choose one for low light
  • A screen, TV or projector
  • Speakers or headphones

You set up the equipment yourself following our guide. We provide the software and help you set it up.

Why it's easy

  • Private. The camera image is never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Touch-free. Nothing to touch — just hands in the air.
  • Clear from the first second. Raise a hand and it sounds. Any visitor can play.
  • Your own world. On request we create a space and music for your venue, brand or exhibition.

Want to try it in your space?

We're running our first pilots now. Tell us where and for whom you'd like the installation — we'll prepare it for your space.
