Handsong — just play

Raise your hand and play: every finger that touches your thumb is a note, and the other hand shapes the sound. Nothing to install — and once opened it works without the internet.

Play

Chrome or Safari, on a laptop or a phone · the camera image never leaves your device

Play

Raise or lower your hand to choose the notes; touch your thumb with any finger and a note sounds — several at once if you like.

Shape the sound

The other hand's fingers are delay, vibrato, tremolo and brightness. Move your hand nearer or farther for more or less reverb.

Choose

14 sounds — leads, keys, winds, strings, three basses — and a different one in each hand; 31 scales in any key.

Share

Record a clip of up to a minute, with the picture and the sound, and send it straight from your phone.
