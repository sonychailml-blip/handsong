- Video placeholder: [Video: people playing with their hands]
- What makes it different:
  - **Hands instead of an instrument.** Bring your thumb to another finger and a note sounds. Raise your hand for a higher note.
  - **Always in tune.** The notes are chosen so you can't play a wrong one: anyone can play.
  - **Living music, not a track.** The sound grows from your movements and never repeats.
  - **Worlds that answer.** Light, particles and space respond to every note.
  - **Nothing to install.** Works in Chrome and Safari on a laptop or a phone.
  - **Private.** The camera image is processed only on your device.

## Night page
- Label: Available · free
- Title: Handsong *Night*
- Lead: Quiet, living music you play with your hands — in a night forest that answers every note with light.
- Button: Play Night
- Small print: Chrome or Safari · a laptop, or a phone on a stand · the camera image never leaves your device
- Who it's for: For anyone who wants a quiet evening, a rest after work, or a play with music without ever having studied it. No notes, no knowledge needed.
- What you do: Raise a hand and bring your thumb to another finger — a note sounds. Higher hand, higher note. Your other hand shapes the echo and the sound, and your distance from the camera shapes the depth of the space.
- What awaits you: A living background music that never repeats, a stream, wind and crickets. Fireflies gather round your light, rings spread on the water, the night slowly changes.
