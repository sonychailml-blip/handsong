# CALM QUALITY BAR (user, RUN 7, 2026-10-17)

Every Calm slice is checked against these ten points; each slice record (WORK-LOG, HANDOFF) names the points it touched and how.

1. **Sound** — organic, rich; no clicks, no harshness, no sudden loudness; soft highs for long headphone sessions; balanced levels between
   timbres and between moods.
2. **Response** — the light follows the hand smoothly at the display rate (interpolated between detections); note onsets immediate.
3. **Nothing sounds wrong** — the mood scales, gentle dynamics, the limiter.
4. **Every gesture answered** — light, glint, sound (micro-feedback).
5. **Beauty and slowness** — eased transitions, nothing abrupt; the forest breathes (fireflies, mist).
6. **Zero friction** — first note within ~10 s of opening; no accounts, no nags; offline; remembers settings.
7. **Reliability** — no freezes, no overheating, polite camera / permission messages.
8. **Depth over time** — a simple start, things to discover (moods, depth, two hands, effects); the forest slowly alive (later: the moon
   moving, a distant bird).
9. **Respect** — no streaks, no pressure; a SLEEP TIMER (15 / 30 / 60 min: the forest and the playing fade out gently) in Calm v1's controls.
10. **Consistency** — one set of fonts, words, spacing and icons everywhere.

## How a slice is checked
- Points that a probe can prove (levels, clicks, onset timing, fades, remembered settings) get a deterministic row (offline render or data).
- Points that need ears or eyes (organic, beauty, slowness) are written as the user's steps, with exactly what to listen or look for.
- A slice that makes a point WORSE (e.g. a louder timbre than the others, an abrupt fade) is not done.
