# Room «Rain» («Дождь») — concept

Status: approved concept, 2026-11-13. Author: the founder with his strategist. This is the brief for the room's plan and every slice that builds it.

## Коротко по-русски
- Вид из окна 8-го этажа в осеннюю дождливую ночь: внизу бульвар с платанами и трамваем, напротив дом в стиле модерн, дальше холмы старого города в огнях (по мотивам Тбилиси).
- Главная идея: **глубина руки = фокус.** Рука близко — огонёк на стекле, резкие капли, город в бокэ. Рука далеко — фокус уезжает в город, капли размываются.
- Ноты: близко — стирают запотевшее стекло, капли вздрагивают; далеко — **зажигают окна** в домах, и в них появляется жизнь. Город запоминает вашу мелодию узором окон.
- Вторая рука: «Волна» — движение города (машины, трамвай, ветер), «Эхо» — сила дождя, «Тепло ↔ Яркость» — цвет света в городе.
- Три настроения со своими ладами, джазовой гармонией фона и новыми тембрами (электропиано, вибрафон, контрабас).
- Стиль: кинематографичный кадр через мокрое стекло, тёплое доминирует, два неоновых акцента, цвет интерфейса — янтарь.
- Комната уникальна: ничего не переносится из леса; общими остаются только движок звука, руки, хост сцены и переключение комнат.

---

## 1. The room in one line
A window on a warm autumn rain at night, high above an old city: you play, and the city answers — windows light up, trams pass, the rain softens or pours.

## 2. The core idea: depth is focus
Night's hands have depth (the distance from the camera). In this room depth is the camera's focus:
- **Hand near** — the light is on the glass. Drops, rivulets, a wet leaf and the windowsill are sharp; the city is soft bokeh. Sound: close and dry, rain tapping on the glass.
- **Hand far** — the light goes through the glass into the city. Focus travels out: the street and the houses sharpen, the drops blur into soft discs. Sound: the street opens, wider echo.
- In between, the focus glides continuously with the hand (eased, never jumpy). The calibration's near/far points map onto glass/city exactly as they map onto near/far in the forest.

## 3. The view
- **Height:** about the 8th floor. The street is alive and readable, the windows opposite are large enough to show small lives, and the city recedes behind.
- **Perspective:** slightly from above and diagonally along the boulevard, not straight at a wall, so the street leads the eye into depth.
- **Layers, near to far:**
  0. **The room's edge (minimal):** an old painted wooden frame with a brass latch, a deep windowsill, a cup of tea with rising steam, a small plant. Nothing more. Dark, lit only by our light and the city's glow.
  1. **The glass:** drops of many sizes, sliding rivulets, a misted border, sometimes a yellow plane leaf stuck to the glass.
  2. **The boulevard below:** wet asphalt with long reflections; plane trees with yellowing leaves; tram tracks and overhead wires; café awnings; street lamps; traffic lights; cars as pairs of headlights and tail lights; people with umbrellas; puddles with rings.
  3. **The house opposite** (the main canvas for play): an Art Nouveau facade — stucco, tall windows, wrought-iron balconies, many windows, each able to hold a small life.
  4. **Roofs:** chimneys with steam, antennas, water tanks, a lit attic window or two.
  5. **The old town on the hills:** carved wooden balconies, church domes, a lit fortress wall, lights climbing the slopes, a TV tower with red lights on the top.
  6. **The sky:** low clouds lit warm by the city from below, deep indigo above; far lightning; aircraft lights.
- **The city is inspired by Tbilisi, not a copy:** no real signage text, no brands, no recognisable real building reproduced exactly.

## 4. Playing
### The playing hand
- **Near (on the glass):** each note wipes a soft clear patch in the mist, as a fingertip would; drops near the light tremble and some slide; a held note keeps the patch clear and lets you "draw" with the light.
- **Far (in the city):** each note **lights a window** where the light points: a lamp turns on behind a curtain and a small life appears (a silhouette reading, someone cooking, a cat on the sill, a plant, a TV's blue flicker). A long note lights a whole floor or switches on a sign.
- **Middle:** a note sends a soft wave of light across the facades.
- **The trace:** windows you lit fade slowly, not at once, so over an evening the facade holds a pattern of the windows you played. The city remembers your melody.
- The fingertip lights refract in the drops when near.

### The effects hand (same three controls as in the forest)
- **Wave:** the city's motion — how many cars, whether trams run, the wind slanting the rain.
- **Echo:** the rain's strength, from drizzle to downpour, with a wider street sound.
- **Warm ↔ Bright:** the colour of the city's light, from warm tungsten to cooler blue and neon.

### The music shown when heard (the bed's events)
- Traffic lights change on the beat of the bed.
- On a chord change, a tram lit from inside passes, sometimes with a blue-white spark on the wire.
- Signs and lit windows breathe gently with long notes.
- Far lightning on the strongest moments, rarely.

## 5. Moods (three, the room's own names)
| Mood | Time and scene | Mode (melody: a safe 5-6 note set from it) | Bed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Late night** («Поздняя ночь») | 2 a.m., drizzle, an empty street, one taxi, a few windows lit | Lydian (dreamy) | sparse, long major-7 chords, electric piano, very soft bass |
| **Evening boulevard** («Вечерний бульвар») | 8 p.m., busy: umbrellas, trams, the café open, many windows | Dorian (warm minor) | m9 and m11 colours, walking-feel bass, vibraphone glints |
| **After the rain** («После ливня») | late, the rain just stopped: puddles shine, mist, neon in the wet asphalt, big slow drops on the glass | Mixolydian or a blues-tinged pentatonic (a little playful) | dominant-9 colours, brushed-texture rhythm (very soft, optional), EP and vibes |

Rules: "you can't play a wrong note" stays true. The jazz comes mainly from the bed's harmony and the timbres, not from risky melody notes.

## 6. The night goes on
Over the session the city falls asleep: windows go out one by one, traffic thins, the rain changes. With the sleep timer the city falls asleep with you, and the last window to go out is one you lit.

**Rare moments** (each with a schedule like the forest's): lightning lights the whole skyline; a cat on a sill opposite; two people under one umbrella; far fireworks over the hills; aircraft lights through the clouds; someone opens a window opposite; a gust sends leaves across the street; a street musician's light under an awning.

## 7. Sound world
- **Near:** rain on the glass (stereo, close), drips from the cornice, a quiet tick in the room.
- **Street:** tyres hissing on wet asphalt, a tram's bell and hum, far thunder, the muffled city.
- Recordings CC0 only (looped without seams, as the stream), or synthesis; levels under the music as in the forest.
- **Timbres for this room (new):** a mellow electric piano (Rhodes-like), a vibraphone, an upright-like bass in the bed; perhaps a soft brushed texture. Played sounds must stay gentle and light on phones.

## 8. Style bible
**Main rule:** warm dominates; cool only in the sky and two accents; darkness makes the contrast.

| Role | Colour |
|---|---|
| Sky, high | deep indigo `#1B1A2B` |
| Clouds' underside (city glow) | warm mauve to amber `#5A3A4A` → `#8A5A44` |
| Facades in shadow | dark umber stone `#2A2220` |
| Plaster in light | ochre `#6B5440` |
| Windows, tungsten | honey `#FFB95E`, amber `#F29A3A` |
| Windows, TV | cool flicker `#7FA6E0` (few) |
| Wet asphalt | blue-black `#121621` with long warm reflections |
| Street lamps | sodium `#FFC27A` |
| Tram spark | blue-white `#DDEBFF` (rare) |
| Accent 1 | pharmacy-cross green `#4FD1A0` |
| Accent 2 | café-sign rose `#E86A92` |
| Plane leaves | muted yellow `#C9963C` |
| Interface tint of this room | amber `#F4C98A` |

- **Image style:** cinematic, as a film frame shot through wet glass: soft bokeh, light over detail, gentle grain. Not photographic realism, not cartoon.
- **Avoid:** cyberpunk neon overload, readable brand text, anything that imitates a real business, cold blue overall.

## 9. Technical direction (for the plan to confirm or replace)
- The camera does not travel: it breathes and leans slightly, as in the forest.
- The city can be layered 2.5D planes with real depth values, thousands of windows as instances (each with its own state), cars and trams as light sprites on paths with reflections in the wet street.
- The rain on glass is a full-screen pass (drops, trails, refraction of what is behind, mist with clearing), at a resolution the phone can afford.
- The focus pull can blend pre-blurred versions of the layers by depth (cheap bokeh) rather than a true depth-of-field pass.
- Phones first: Standard must be smooth on a mid Android and an iPhone; Detailed adds detail. A file budget for this room of its own (e.g. 15 MB).
- AI-generated images are allowed for painted layers (rule #32: tool and terms registered); CC0/CC-BY/own recordings and art otherwise.

## 10. Decided
- Each room is unique: nothing is moved or copied out of the forest into a shared kit. Shared stays only what is shared today: the sound engine, the hands, the scene host, the room record and switching, the music-bed machinery, settings.
- Autumn, warm rain. Three moods with their own modes, bed recipes and timbres.
- The room's own start still and reveal, its own interface tint (amber).
- Until the founder approves the room, it stays out of the players' edition (no Room button for players).
